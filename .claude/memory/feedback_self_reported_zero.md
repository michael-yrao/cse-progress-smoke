**When the learner says they don't have a topic down, plan for the worst case: they kept *none* of it.**
Set by the learner Aug 8, 2026, immediately after flagging networking fact 2 as unfinished:

> *"when I say that, you can assume worst case scenario that I kept 0 of the knowledge"*

**So a self-report of "not done" is not a partial credit statement.** Do not split the topic into "the
part we worked" and "the part that's left" and then teach only the remainder. Re-open it from the top.
The pieces I recorded as *covered* are exactly the pieces most likely to be mis-recorded, because what I
actually observed was **the learner following along in the moment**, which is not retention — it is the
same distinction as [[feedback_coding_rules]] (recognition ≠ execution) and the teach/measure split.

**Why the learner is right to force the worst case, and why it's cheap:**

- **The failure modes are wildly asymmetric.** Re-covering something they *did* retain costs a few
  minutes and they will say so ("yep, got this") — it self-corrects instantly. Building on a foundation
  that isn't there costs the whole session: every later step silently rests on a gap, they get lost with
  no idea which link broke, and the rating that follows is measuring my assumption, not their recall.
- **My "covered" list is evidence about ME, not about them.** It records what was *said in the room*.
  A session log is a record of teaching delivered, never of learning retained — the Aug 8 *"a note
  exists ≠ the learner has been taught"* lesson, one level further in.
- **It costs nothing to check.** Opening with *"quick — what does TCP actually add to IP?"* takes one
  turn and replaces the whole guess.

**How to apply:**

1. **On "I don't have X down" → treat X as never-encoded in full.** Schedule a teach (§2a), not a
   top-up, and **unrated** — a rep on material they just told you they don't have measures nothing.
2. **Re-open from the first fact, including anything logged as already worked.** Move fast through what
   they confirm; do not skip it unasked.
3. **Never rate anything covering it** until it has been re-taught *and* had a gap to forget in. If a
   cold rep would span both retained and unretained material, **scope it to the retained part** —
   firing the rest cold manufactures a 🔴 and a short-interval churn loop on material that was never
   encoded. (This once named the Recall Card sprint, retired Aug 13, 2026; the rule applies to any
   cold rep, and a **mock interview** is the live example.)
4. **Update the written note's "worked so far" claim** rather than leaving it to be believed later. A
   stale coverage claim is how this recurs a month from now with no one left who remembers.
5. **Applies to any self-report of not-having-it** — *"I don't remember this"*, *"this never stuck"*,
   *"I'd have to look it up"*. Take it literally and at full strength. It is self-reported like Comfort
   is, and the learner's call is final ([[feedback_infer_comfort]]).

**The one thing this does NOT license:** re-teaching by *explanation dump*. Spine-then-pull still binds
([[feedback_interactive_learning]]) — small bit, stop, they pull. Worst-case assumption changes **how
much** to cover, never **how** to cover it.
