When the learner is struggling with a technique, do not offer a no-code drill or a detached exercise.
The remedy is the next real rep of the problem, with their questions answered in full as they come up.

**Why:** after two heavily coached reps of the same technique, the learner said it was "giving me a really
really hard time". The coach offered a no-code "list the root's children" drill twice. Learner: "i dont feel
like that helps, doing these problems that i dont understand repeatedly helps more and makes me ask
questions the more i do them."

**How to apply:** Name where the rep broke, answer what they ask, and let the spaced-repetition
schedule bring the problem back (a 🔴 returns on the Blank interval). Do not propose a substitute
activity. Related: [[feedback_let_learner_pace]], [[feedback_interactive_learning]].
