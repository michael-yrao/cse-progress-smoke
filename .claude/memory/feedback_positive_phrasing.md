Write rules and guidance as what to do. Use *never* or *don't* only when no positive wording carries the meaning. Order a day's problems by priority, highest first, whenever a schedule or board is shown. Build each day toward the ceiling, since it is the goal; a small overshoot is fine.

**Why:** the learner reads the rule text and the boards; negative framing and an unordered day both cost them a second read. The ceiling is the daily goal (`effort_budget.ceiling`), so a day built well under it wastes capacity.
**How to apply:** skill/rule wording, board and schedule row order, and the weekly build's day totals. Operational copies: `references/weekly-build.md` (Row order), `references/scaffolding.md` (board order), `references/effort-budget.md` (ceiling is the goal).
